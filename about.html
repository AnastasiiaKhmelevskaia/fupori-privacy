Meet Fupori

Small habits.
A little company.

Fupori is an iPhone habit tracker with companions to cheer you on. Choose a routine, give it a schedule, and see what your small steps add up to.

See how it works ↓

A routine that feels like yours.

Start with one habit. Fupori gives it a place in your day and a companion to grow alongside it.

01 / CREATE

Pick your habit

Choose a starter habit or name your own. Set it for every day, every few days, or the weekdays that work for you.

02 / REMEMBER

Make room for it

Add reminders when they help. Your schedule and progress stay easy to find in the app.

03 / GROW

Keep each other going

Complete habits to make progress with your companion. Earn care rewards and watch your shared journey grow.
