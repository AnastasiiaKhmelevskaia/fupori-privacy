01

Data in Fupori

Fupori stores the information you enter or create while using the app, including habit names and schedules, reminders, completion history, companion progress, achievements, and care rewards. It also saves app preferences such as your display name, theme, notification choice, and first-use date.

This information is stored on your device. The production app uses Apple's CloudKit to sync habit and progress records through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. Apple stores that synced data in your iCloud account. The developer cannot view your private database in the CloudKit developer portal.

The app's widget reads a small snapshot shared locally between the app and its widget. Habit reminders are scheduled as notifications on your device.

02

Services we use

PostHog analytics

In the production app, PostHog receives an app_launched event with the app environment. The event also carries information normally added by the PostHog SDK, such as an SDK-generated identifier, event time, and app or device details. We use this to understand app launches. Fupori does not intentionally send habit names or completion history to PostHog. Automatic screen capture, control capture, and session replay are disabled.

Sentry diagnostics

Sentry receives error and crash reports, logs, performance traces, and profiling data so we can diagnose problems. Reports can include device and app details, an IP address, and information contained in error messages or logs. Because the app currently enables Sentry's default personal information setting, diagnostic reports may contain personal information.

Apple iCloud

CloudKit handles sync for the app's private iCloud data. You can use Fupori without an app account; iCloud sync depends on your device's Apple Account and iCloud settings.

These providers process data for the purposes described above. You can read Apple's privacy policy, PostHog's privacy policy, and Sentry's privacy policy for information about their practices.

03

Your choices

You can edit or delete habits in Fupori. You can turn off habit reminders in the app or change notification permission in iOS Settings. You can manage Fupori's iCloud access in your device's iCloud settings.

The current app has no in-app switch for PostHog analytics or Sentry diagnostics. To stop new data from being sent by the app, stop using it or remove it from your device. If you have a privacy request about data already sent, use the contact link below.

04

Retention and deletion

Removing Fupori deletes its local app data from that device. If you used iCloud sync, copies can remain in your private iCloud storage and on your other devices. You can manage that storage through your Apple Account's iCloud settings.

PostHog and Sentry keep information according to the settings of the services we use and their applicable retention policies. Contact us to ask about access to or deletion of information held through those services. We may need enough information to find the relevant records, and some requests may be limited by what can be identified or by legal requirements.

05

This website

This page is a static website. It does not load advertising or analytics scripts and does not set cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the page; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

06

Contact and updates

For a privacy question or request, open a GitHub issue. Issues are public, so please share only a short description and no habit content, device identifiers, or other personal information. We can arrange a private way to continue if needed.
